Appendix B — Useful Formulae

This appendix collects a list of useful equations, identities, and constants that we use throughout the notes. We use SI units throughout. See Chapter 18 for how to translate equations written in Gaussian units.

B.1 Vector Algebra and Calculus

Products of vectors: \[ \begin{aligned} \mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}) &= \mathbf{b} \cdot (\mathbf{c} \times \mathbf{a}) = \mathbf{c} \cdot (\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}), \\ \mathbf{a} \times (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c}) &= \mathbf{b} (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c}) - \mathbf{c} (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}). \end{aligned} \tag{B.1}\]

Product rules: \[ \begin{aligned} \nabla (f g) &= f \nabla g + g \nabla f, \\ \nabla \cdot (f \mathbf{A}) &= f \nabla \cdot \mathbf{A} + \mathbf{A} \cdot \nabla f, \\ \nabla \times (f \mathbf{A}) &= f \nabla \times \mathbf{A} + \nabla f \times \mathbf{A}, \\ \nabla \cdot (\mathbf{A} \times \mathbf{B}) &= \mathbf{B} \cdot (\nabla \times \mathbf{A}) - \mathbf{A} \cdot (\nabla \times \mathbf{B}), \end{aligned} \tag{B.2}\] \[ \begin{aligned} \nabla (\mathbf{A} \cdot \mathbf{B}) &= \mathbf{A} \times (\nabla \times \mathbf{B}) + \mathbf{B} \times (\nabla \times \mathbf{A}) \\ & \qquad {} + (\mathbf{A} \cdot \nabla) \mathbf{B} + (\mathbf{B} \cdot \nabla) \mathbf{A}, \\ \nabla \times (\mathbf{A} \times \mathbf{B}) &= \mathbf{A} (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B}) - \mathbf{B} (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{A}) \\ & \qquad {} + (\mathbf{B} \cdot \nabla) \mathbf{A} - (\mathbf{A} \cdot \nabla) \mathbf{B}. \end{aligned} \tag{B.3}\]

Second derivatives: \[ \begin{aligned} \nabla \times \nabla f &= 0, \qquad \nabla \cdot (\nabla \times \mathbf{A}) = 0, \\ \nabla \cdot \nabla f &= \nabla^2 f, \\ \nabla \times (\nabla \times \mathbf{A}) &= \nabla (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{A}) - \nabla^2 \mathbf{A}. \end{aligned} \tag{B.4}\]

Two combinations that appear often: \[ \begin{aligned} (\mathbf{v} \cdot \nabla) \mathbf{v} &= \nabla \left( \frac{1}{2} v^2 \right) - \mathbf{v} \times (\nabla \times \mathbf{v}), \\ (\nabla \times \mathbf{B}) \times \mathbf{B} &= (\mathbf{B} \cdot \nabla) \mathbf{B} - \nabla \left( \frac{1}{2} B^2 \right). \end{aligned} \tag{B.5}\]

Tensors. We use \((\nabla \mathbf{A})_{ij} = \partial_j A_i\), \((\nabla \cdot \mathbf{T})_i = \sum_j \partial_j T_{ij}\), \((\mathbf{A} \otimes \mathbf{B})_{ij} = A_i B_j\), and \(\mathbf{T} : \mathbf{S} = \sum_{i,j} T_{ij} S_{ji}\) (Appendix A). Then, for a symmetric tensor \(\mathbf{T}\), \[ \begin{aligned} \nabla \cdot (\mathbf{A} \otimes \mathbf{B}) &= \mathbf{A} (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B}) + (\mathbf{B} \cdot \nabla) \mathbf{A}, \\ \nabla \cdot (f \mathbf{I}) &= \nabla f, \\ \nabla \cdot (\mathbf{T} \cdot \mathbf{v}) &= (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{T}) \cdot \mathbf{v} + \mathbf{T} : \nabla \mathbf{v}. \end{aligned} \tag{B.6}\]

The position vector \(\mathbf{r}\), with \(r = |\mathbf{r}|\): \[ \begin{aligned} \nabla r &= \hat{\mathbf{r}}, \qquad \nabla \cdot \mathbf{r} = 3, \\ \nabla \times \mathbf{r} &= 0, \qquad \nabla \mathbf{r} = \mathbf{I}, \\ \nabla \frac{1}{r} &= - \frac{\hat{\mathbf{r}}}{r^2}, \qquad \nabla^2 \frac{1}{r} = - 4 \pi \delta (\mathbf{r}). \end{aligned} \tag{B.7}\]

Integral theorems. For a region \(\Omega\) with boundary \(\partial \Omega\) and outward normal \(\mathbf{n}\), and for a surface \(A\) bounded by the curve \(\partial A\): \[ \begin{aligned} \int_{\Omega} \nabla \cdot \mathbf{F} \mathrm{d} V &= \oint_{\partial \Omega} \mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{n} \mathrm{d} A, \\ \int_{\Omega} \nabla f \mathrm{d} V &= \oint_{\partial \Omega} f \mathbf{n} \mathrm{d} A, \\ \int_{\Omega} \nabla \cdot \mathbf{T} \mathrm{d} V &= \oint_{\partial \Omega} \mathbf{T} \cdot \mathbf{n} \mathrm{d} A, \\ \int_A (\nabla \times \mathbf{F}) \cdot \mathrm{d} \mathbf{A} &= \oint_{\partial A} \mathbf{F} \cdot \mathrm{d} \mathbf{l}. \end{aligned} \tag{B.8}\] For a region \(\Omega (t)\) moving with the fluid (Reynolds transport theorem): \[ \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d} t} \int_{\Omega (t)} f \mathrm{d} V = \int_{\Omega (t)} \frac{\partial f}{\partial t} \mathrm{d} V + \oint_{\partial \Omega (t)} f \mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{n} \mathrm{d} A. \tag{B.9}\]

B.2 Curvilinear Coordinates

B.2.1 Cylindrical coordinates \((R, \phi, z)\)

\(x = R \cos \phi\), \(y = R \sin \phi\), \(\mathrm{d} V = R \mathrm{d} R \mathrm{d} \phi \mathrm{d} z\), \(\partial_{\phi} \hat{\mathbf{R}} = \hat{\boldsymbol{\phi}}\), \(\partial_{\phi} \hat{\boldsymbol{\phi}} = - \hat{\mathbf{R}}\). \[ \begin{aligned} \nabla f &= \partial_R f \, \hat{\mathbf{R}} + \frac{1}{R} \partial_{\phi} f \, \hat{\boldsymbol{\phi}} + \partial_z f \, \hat{\mathbf{z}}, \\ \nabla \cdot \mathbf{A} &= \frac{1}{R} \partial_R (R A_R) + \frac{1}{R} \partial_{\phi} A_{\phi} + \partial_z A_z, \\ \nabla^2 f &= \frac{1}{R} \partial_R (R \, \partial_R f) + \frac{1}{R^2} \partial_{\phi}^2 f + \partial_z^2 f, \end{aligned} \tag{B.10}\] \[ \begin{aligned} \nabla \times \mathbf{A} &= \left[ \frac{1}{R} \partial_{\phi} A_z - \partial_z A_{\phi} \right] \hat{\mathbf{R}} \\ & \qquad {} + [\partial_z A_R - \partial_R A_z] \hat{\boldsymbol{\phi}} \\ & \qquad {} + \frac{1}{R} [\partial_R (R A_{\phi}) - \partial_{\phi} A_R] \hat{\mathbf{z}}. \end{aligned} \tag{B.11}\] With \(\mathbf{A} \cdot \nabla = A_R \partial_R + \frac{A_{\phi}}{R} \partial_{\phi} + A_z \partial_z\) acting on the components: \[ \begin{aligned} [(\mathbf{A} \cdot \nabla) \mathbf{B}]_R &= \mathbf{A} \cdot \nabla B_R - \frac{A_{\phi} B_{\phi}}{R}, \\ [(\mathbf{A} \cdot \nabla) \mathbf{B}]_{\phi} &= \mathbf{A} \cdot \nabla B_{\phi} + \frac{A_{\phi} B_R}{R}, \\ [(\mathbf{A} \cdot \nabla) \mathbf{B}]_z &= \mathbf{A} \cdot \nabla B_z, \end{aligned} \tag{B.12}\] \[ \begin{aligned} (\nabla^2 \mathbf{A})_R &= \nabla^2 A_R - \frac{A_R}{R^2} - \frac{2}{R^2} \partial_{\phi} A_{\phi}, \\ (\nabla^2 \mathbf{A})_{\phi} &= \nabla^2 A_{\phi} - \frac{A_{\phi}}{R^2} + \frac{2}{R^2} \partial_{\phi} A_R, \\ (\nabla^2 \mathbf{A})_z &= \nabla^2 A_z. \end{aligned} \tag{B.13}\]

B.2.2 Spherical coordinates \((r, \theta, \phi)\)

\(x = r \sin \theta \cos \phi\), \(y = r \sin \theta \sin \phi\), \(z = r \cos \theta\), \(\mathrm{d} V = r^2 \sin \theta \mathrm{d} r \mathrm{d} \theta \mathrm{d} \phi\), \(\hat{\mathbf{z}} = \cos \theta \, \hat{\mathbf{r}} - \sin \theta \, \hat{\boldsymbol{\theta}}\). \[ \begin{aligned} \nabla f &= \partial_r f \, \hat{\mathbf{r}} + \frac{1}{r} \partial_{\theta} f \, \hat{\boldsymbol{\theta}} + \frac{1}{r \sin \theta} \partial_{\phi} f \, \hat{\boldsymbol{\phi}}, \\ \nabla \cdot \mathbf{A} &= \frac{1}{r^2} \partial_r (r^2 A_r) + \frac{1}{r \sin \theta} \partial_{\theta} (\sin \theta A_{\theta}) \\ & \qquad {} + \frac{1}{r \sin \theta} \partial_{\phi} A_{\phi}, \\ \nabla^2 f &= \frac{1}{r^2} \partial_r (r^2 \partial_r f) + \frac{1}{r^2 \sin \theta} \partial_{\theta} (\sin \theta \, \partial_{\theta} f) \\ & \qquad {} + \frac{1}{r^2 \sin^2 \theta} \partial_{\phi}^2 f, \end{aligned} \tag{B.14}\] \[ \begin{aligned} \nabla \times \mathbf{A} &= \frac{1}{r \sin \theta} [\partial_{\theta} (\sin \theta A_{\phi}) - \partial_{\phi} A_{\theta}] \hat{\mathbf{r}} \\ & \qquad {} + \frac{1}{r} \left[ \frac{1}{\sin \theta} \partial_{\phi} A_r - \partial_r (r A_{\phi}) \right] \hat{\boldsymbol{\theta}} \\ & \qquad {} + \frac{1}{r} [\partial_r (r A_{\theta}) - \partial_{\theta} A_r] \hat{\boldsymbol{\phi}}. \end{aligned} \tag{B.15}\] With \(\mathbf{A} \cdot \nabla = A_r \partial_r + \frac{A_{\theta}}{r} \partial_{\theta} + \frac{A_{\phi}}{r \sin \theta} \partial_{\phi}\) acting on the components: \[ \begin{aligned} [(\mathbf{A} \cdot \nabla) \mathbf{B}]_r &= \mathbf{A} \cdot \nabla B_r - \frac{A_{\theta} B_{\theta} + A_{\phi} B_{\phi}}{r}, \\ [(\mathbf{A} \cdot \nabla) \mathbf{B}]_{\theta} &= \mathbf{A} \cdot \nabla B_{\theta} + \frac{A_{\theta} B_r}{r} \\ & \qquad {} - \frac{A_{\phi} B_{\phi} \cot \theta}{r}, \\ [(\mathbf{A} \cdot \nabla) \mathbf{B}]_{\phi} &= \mathbf{A} \cdot \nabla B_{\phi} + \frac{A_{\phi} B_r}{r} \\ & \qquad {} + \frac{A_{\phi} B_{\theta} \cot \theta}{r}. \end{aligned} \tag{B.16}\]

B.3 Thermodynamics

Composition: mass density, mean molecular weight, and mass fractions, with \(Y_i = n_i / n\) the particle number fractions, \[ \rho = \mu m_u n, \qquad \mu = \sum_i Y_i \frac{m_i}{m_u}, \qquad X_i = \frac{\rho_i}{\rho}. \tag{B.17}\] First law, also per unit mass with \(v = 1/\rho\), and the specific enthalpy \(h\): \[ \begin{aligned} \mathrm{d} E &= T \mathrm{d} S - p \mathrm{d} V, \\ \mathrm{d} \varepsilon &= T \mathrm{d} s - p \mathrm{d} v = T \mathrm{d} s + \frac{p}{\rho^2} \mathrm{d} \rho, \\ h &= \varepsilon + \frac{p}{\rho}, \qquad \mathrm{d} h = T \mathrm{d} s + \frac{\mathrm{d} p}{\rho}. \end{aligned} \tag{B.18}\] Ideal gas and \(\Gamma\)-law equation of state, with \(u = \rho \varepsilon\) the internal energy density (\(\Gamma = 5/3\) for a monatomic gas, \(7/5\) for a diatomic gas, \(4/3\) for radiation): \[ p = n k_B T = \frac{\rho k_B T}{\mu m_u}, \qquad p = (\Gamma - 1) \rho \varepsilon = (\Gamma - 1) u. \tag{B.19}\] Adiabatic changes, with \(K\) a function of the entropy only, and the polytropic equation of state: \[ p = K \rho^{\Gamma}, \qquad p = K \rho^{1 + 1 / n}. \tag{B.20}\] Adiabatic and isothermal sound speeds: \[ c_s^2 = \left( \frac{\partial p}{\partial \rho} \right)_s, \qquad c_{\mathrm{iso}}^2 = \left( \frac{\partial p}{\partial \rho} \right)_T. \tag{B.21}\] Radiation: energy density, pressure, the flux from a blackbody, and the Planck function: \[ \begin{aligned} u &= a T^4, \qquad p = \frac{1}{3} u, \\ a &= \frac{4 \sigma}{c}, \qquad F = \sigma T^4, \\ B_{\nu} &= \frac{2 h \nu^3}{c^2} \frac{1}{\mathrm{e}^{h \nu / k_B T} - 1}. \end{aligned} \tag{B.22}\] Mean free path, with \(\sigma\) the cross section and \(\kappa\) the opacity: \[ \lambda = \frac{1}{n \sigma} = \frac{1}{\kappa \rho}. \tag{B.23}\]

B.4 Hydrodynamics and Gravity

Rate of change following a fluid element: \[ \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d} t} = \frac{\partial}{\partial t} + \mathbf{v} \cdot \nabla. \tag{B.24}\] Equations of hydrodynamics in Lagrangian form, with \(\mathbf{f}\) the external force per unit volume and \(\dot{q}\) the heating rate per unit mass: \[ \begin{aligned} \frac{1}{\rho} \frac{\mathrm{d} \rho}{\mathrm{d} t} &= - \nabla \cdot \mathbf{v}, \\ \rho \frac{\mathrm{d} \mathbf{v}}{\mathrm{d} t} &= - \nabla p + \mathbf{f}, \\ \rho \frac{\mathrm{d} \varepsilon}{\mathrm{d} t} &= \rho \dot{q} - p \nabla \cdot \mathbf{v}. \end{aligned} \tag{B.25}\] In conservation form, \(\partial_t U + \nabla \cdot \mathbf{F} = S\): \[ \begin{aligned} & \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot (\rho \mathbf{v}) = 0, \\ & \frac{\partial (\rho \mathbf{v})}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot [\rho \mathbf{v} \otimes \mathbf{v} + p \mathbf{I}] = \mathbf{f}, \\ & \frac{\partial}{\partial t} \left[ \rho \left( \varepsilon + \frac{1}{2} v^2 \right) \right] \\ & \quad {} + \nabla \cdot \left[ \left( \rho \varepsilon + \frac{1}{2} \rho v^2 + p \right) \mathbf{v} \right] = \rho \dot{q} + \mathbf{f} \cdot \mathbf{v}. \end{aligned} \tag{B.26}\] Gravity: potential, Poisson equation, and gravitational energy: \[ \begin{aligned} \mathbf{g} &= - \nabla \Psi, \qquad \nabla^2 \Psi = 4 \pi G \rho, \\ \Psi (\mathbf{r}) &= - G \int \frac{\rho (\mathbf{r}')}{|\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{r}'|} \mathrm{d}^3 r', \\ W &= \frac{1}{2} \int \rho \Psi \mathrm{d} V. \end{aligned} \tag{B.27}\] Hydrostatic equilibrium, and the virial theorem for a system in equilibrium, with \(K\) the kinetic energy: \[ \nabla p = - \rho \nabla \Psi, \qquad 2 K + W + 3 \int p \mathrm{d} V = 0. \tag{B.28}\] Vorticity equation and Bernoulli’s constant, with \(\mathcal{H} = \int \mathrm{d} p / \rho\) for a barotropic gas: \[ \begin{aligned} \boldsymbol{\omega} &= \nabla \times \mathbf{v}, \qquad \mathcal{B} = \frac{1}{2} v^2 + \mathcal{H} + \Psi, \\ \frac{\partial \boldsymbol{\omega}}{\partial t} &= \nabla \times (\mathbf{v} \times \boldsymbol{\omega}) + \frac{1}{\rho^2} \nabla \rho \times \nabla p. \end{aligned} \tag{B.29}\]

Linear perturbations. The Eulerian perturbation \(q'\) and the Lagrangian perturbation \(\delta q\) of a quantity \(q\) are related by \(q' = \delta q - \boldsymbol{\xi} \cdot \nabla q_0\), where \(\boldsymbol{\xi}\) is the displacement. Plane waves go as \(\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i} (\mathbf{k} \cdot \mathbf{x} - \omega t)}\), so that \[ \begin{aligned} & \nabla \to \mathrm{i} \mathbf{k}, \qquad \frac{\partial}{\partial t} \to - \mathrm{i} \omega, \\ & v_{\mathrm{ph}} = \frac{\omega}{k}, \qquad \mathbf{v}_g = \frac{\partial \omega}{\partial \mathbf{k}}. \end{aligned} \tag{B.30}\]

Jump conditions for a conservation law \(\partial_t U + \partial_x F = 0\) across a discontinuity moving with speed \(s\), with \(\mathrm{L}\) and \(\mathrm{R}\) the states on its left and right: \[ s (U_{\mathrm{L}} - U_{\mathrm{R}}) = F (U_{\mathrm{L}}) - F (U_{\mathrm{R}}). \tag{B.31}\] Across a steady shock, in the shock frame, with 1 and 2 the upstream and downstream states: \[ \begin{aligned} \rho_1 u_1 &= \rho_2 u_2, \\ p_1 + \rho_1 u_1^2 &= p_2 + \rho_2 u_2^2, \\ h_1 + \frac{1}{2} u_1^2 &= h_2 + \frac{1}{2} u_2^2. \end{aligned} \tag{B.32}\]

B.5 Diffusion and Viscosity

Diffusion equation: \[ \frac{\partial P}{\partial t} = \nabla \cdot (D \nabla P). \tag{B.33}\] Rate-of-strain tensor, viscous stress tensor, and Navier–Stokes equation: \[ \begin{aligned} \mathbf{S} &= \frac{1}{2} (\nabla \mathbf{v} + \nabla \mathbf{v}^T), \\ \boldsymbol{\tau} &= \zeta (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{v}) \mathbf{I} \\ & \qquad {} + \rho \nu \left[ \nabla \mathbf{v} + \nabla \mathbf{v}^T - \frac{2}{3} (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{v}) \mathbf{I} \right], \\ \rho \frac{\mathrm{d} \mathbf{v}}{\mathrm{d} t} &= - \nabla p + \nabla \cdot \boldsymbol{\tau} + \mathbf{f}. \end{aligned} \tag{B.34}\] Reynolds number, for a flow with velocity \(V\) on a scale \(L\): \[ \mathrm{Re} = \frac{L V}{\nu}. \tag{B.35}\] Thin disks: surface density, Keplerian angular velocity, scale height, and \(\alpha\) viscosity: \[ \begin{aligned} & \Sigma = \int \rho \mathrm{d} z, \qquad \Omega = \left( \frac{G M}{R^3} \right)^{1 / 2}, \\ & H = \frac{c_s}{\Omega}, \qquad \nu = \alpha c_s H. \end{aligned} \tag{B.36}\]

B.6 Electromagnetism and Magnetohydrodynamics

Maxwell’s equations, with \(q\) the charge density and \(\mathbf{j}\) the current density, and the Lorentz force on a particle of charge \(Q\): \[ \begin{aligned} \nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} &= \frac{q}{\epsilon_0}, \qquad \nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0, \\ \nabla \times \mathbf{E} &= - \frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t}, \\ \nabla \times \mathbf{B} &= \mu_0 \mathbf{j} + \mu_0 \epsilon_0 \frac{\partial \mathbf{E}}{\partial t}, \\ \mathbf{F} &= Q (\mathbf{E} + \mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B}), \qquad \mu_0 \epsilon_0 = \frac{1}{c^2}. \end{aligned} \tag{B.37}\] Debye length, plasma frequency, and Larmor radius: \[ \lambda_D = \sqrt{\frac{\epsilon_0 k_B T}{n e^2}}, \qquad \omega_p = \sqrt{\frac{n e^2}{\epsilon_0 m_e}}, \qquad r_L = \frac{m v_{\perp}}{|Q| B}. \tag{B.38}\] Ohm’s law, Ampère’s law without displacement current, magnetic diffusivity, and the induction equation: \[ \begin{aligned} \mathbf{j} &= \sigma (\mathbf{E} + \mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B}), \qquad \nabla \times \mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \mathbf{j}, \\ \eta &= \frac{1}{\mu_0 \sigma}, \\ \frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t} &= \nabla \times (\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B}) - \nabla \times (\eta \nabla \times \mathbf{B}). \end{aligned} \tag{B.39}\] Magnetic Reynolds number: \[ \mathrm{R}_M = \frac{L V}{\eta}. \tag{B.40}\] Equations of ideal MHD (\(\eta = 0\), so that \(\mathbf{E} = - \mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B}\)) in conservation form: \[ \begin{aligned} & \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot (\rho \mathbf{v}) = 0, \\ & \frac{\partial (\rho \mathbf{v})}{\partial t} \\ & \qquad {} + \nabla \cdot \biggl[ \rho \mathbf{v} \otimes \mathbf{v} + \left( p + \frac{B^2}{2 \mu_0} \right) \mathbf{I} \\ & \qquad\qquad {} - \frac{\mathbf{B} \otimes \mathbf{B}}{\mu_0} \biggr] = \mathbf{f}, \\ & \frac{\partial}{\partial t} \left[ \rho \left( \varepsilon + \frac{1}{2} v^2 \right) + \frac{B^2}{2 \mu_0} \right] \\ & \qquad {} + \nabla \cdot \biggl[ \left( \rho \varepsilon + \frac{1}{2} \rho v^2 + p \right) \mathbf{v} \\ & \qquad\qquad {} + \frac{\mathbf{B} \times (\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B})}{\mu_0} \biggr] \\ & \qquad = \rho \dot{q} + \mathbf{f} \cdot \mathbf{v}, \\ & \frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t} = \nabla \times (\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B}). \end{aligned} \tag{B.41}\] The momentum equation in Lagrangian form, and the magnetic force split into tension and pressure: \[ \begin{aligned} \rho \frac{\mathrm{d} \mathbf{v}}{\mathrm{d} t} &= \mathbf{j} \times \mathbf{B} - \nabla p + \mathbf{f}, \\ \mathbf{j} \times \mathbf{B} &= \frac{(\mathbf{B} \cdot \nabla) \mathbf{B}}{\mu_0} - \nabla \frac{B^2}{2 \mu_0}. \end{aligned} \tag{B.42}\] Plasma beta and Alfvén speed: \[ \beta = \frac{2 \mu_0 p}{B^2}, \qquad v_A = \frac{B}{\sqrt{\mu_0 \rho}}. \tag{B.43}\]

B.7 Mathematics

Expansions for \(|x| \ll 1\): \[ \begin{aligned} (1 + x)^{\alpha} &\approx 1 + \alpha x, \qquad \mathrm{e}^x \approx 1 + x + \frac{x^2}{2}, \\ \ln (1 + x) &\approx x - \frac{x^2}{2}, \qquad \sin x \approx x - \frac{x^3}{6}, \\ \cos x &\approx 1 - \frac{x^2}{2}, \qquad \tanh x \approx x. \end{aligned} \tag{B.44}\] Roots of a quadratic, and the solution of the harmonic oscillator: \[ \begin{aligned} a x^2 + b x + c = 0 & \;\Rightarrow\; x = \frac{- b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4 a c}}{2 a}, \\ \ddot{x} = - \omega^2 x & \;\Rightarrow\; x = A \cos \omega t + B \sin \omega t. \end{aligned} \tag{B.45}\] Some integrals: \[ \begin{aligned} & \int_{- \infty}^{\infty} \mathrm{e}^{- x^2} \mathrm{d} x = \sqrt{\pi}, \qquad \int \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{\cosh^2 x} = \tanh x, \\ & \int_0^{\pi} \sin \theta \cos^2 \theta \mathrm{d} \theta = \frac{2}{3}. \end{aligned} \tag{B.46}\]
